MINING DISCOVERY - COMPANY KNOWLEDGE

ABOUT MINING DISCOVERY

Mining Discovery is a mining-focused media and marketing platform.

Mining Discovery provides mining news, insights, analysis, company
information and marketing services for the global mining community.

The platform focuses on areas including mining news, exploration,
production, regulation, investment, sustainability, investor relations,
innovation and industry developments.

Mining Discovery aims to connect mining companies, investors,
professionals, regulators, communities and other stakeholders.

Mining Discovery was founded in 2022.

The company describes its purpose as:
- Illuminating the mining industry
- Turning insight into action
- Fostering transparency
- Building bridges between mining companies, investors, regulators
  and communities

MINING DISCOVERY SERVICES

Mining Discovery offers the following services:

1. Digital Branding
Mining Discovery helps mining and industrial companies develop
brand identities and improve brand visibility and recognition.

2. Social Media Marketing
Mining Discovery develops social media campaigns and strategies
designed to strengthen a company's brand voice and engagement.

3. Google Ads & Paid Campaigns
Mining Discovery creates and manages Google Ads and paid campaigns
designed to generate qualified traffic and measurable results.

4. LinkedIn & Meta Ads
Mining Discovery creates and manages advertising campaigns on
LinkedIn and Meta for professional and broader audience reach.

5. Logo & Visual Design
Mining Discovery creates visual identities and logos for companies
in the mining industry.

6. Public Relations (PR)
Mining Discovery provides media relations, press releases,
thought leadership and publicity support.

7. Webinars & Events
Mining Discovery organizes and supports webinars and virtual events
for education, engagement and industry positioning.

8. Website Development
Mining Discovery develops websites designed to be user-friendly,
fast and suitable for businesses and organizations.

9. App Development
Mining Discovery develops web and mobile applications designed to
improve customer engagement and operational efficiency.
